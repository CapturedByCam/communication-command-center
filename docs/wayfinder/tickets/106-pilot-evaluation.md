# Approve pilot accuracy thresholds and review sample

## Question

What sample and thresholds determine whether the pilot is useful enough to harden?

## Type

Grilling

## Status

Closed

## Resolution

Use at least 50 sanitized or explicitly approved examples covering leads, active projects, aviation, administrative, personal, acknowledgements, newsletters, complaints, pricing, deadlines, and promises. Require at least 90% waiting-state accuracy, 90% actionable precision, zero raw-content retention violations, zero auto-sends, and at least 80% minor-or-no-edit routine drafts.
